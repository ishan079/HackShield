The day before a session, people gather for an open discussion about what’s coming. Sometimes it’s an hour; sometimes the conversation goes on for several.

That shared curiosity carries into the next day: focused teaching, questions, resources on Discord and WhatsApp, and hands-on learning that gives the ideas somewhere to land.
